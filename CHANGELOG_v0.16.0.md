# Brainrot Academy v0.16.0 — Content Depth + Brain Battle 2.0

## Brain Battle 2.0
- Added a real combat HUD with learner Brain Shield HP and Glorp HP.
- Correct answers now deal damage to Glorp.
- Streak combat tiers: Brain Hit, 3+ Combo Hit, 5+ Power Attack, and 10+ Professor Special.
- Wrong answers trigger Glorp counterattacks, but the learner's Brain Shield has a safety floor so academic struggle never becomes a punitive game-over.
- Added combat callouts, HP animation, KO presentation, particles, haptics, and existing character reaction integration.

## Content depth
- Added a new supplemental curriculum pack with 336 authored items across Grades 1–6.
- Expanded vocabulary, spelling, reading, grammar, science, geography, and general/life-skills content by eight authored items per grade per subject.
- Authored curriculum now exceeds 1,400 items before generated math/time/calendar variations are counted.
- Reading expansion includes paired comprehension questions on shared short passages to support deeper reading work instead of one-question disposable passages.

## Learning intelligence
- Missed skills can resurface later in the same session through a delayed review queue.
- Added short cross-session recent-question memory for single-subject sessions to reduce repetitive prompts.
- Progress schema v5 records resurfaced-question accuracy separately while preserving existing mastery totals.
