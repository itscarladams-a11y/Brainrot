# Brainrot Academy v0.16.7 — Parts of Speech Lab

## New: dedicated Parts of Speech course

- Added **Professor Brainrot's Parts of Speech Lab** as a direct activity and a dedicated Grammar silo.
- Eight sequenced lessons: Nouns, Verbs, Adjectives, Pronouns, Adverbs, Prepositions, Conjunctions, and Interjections.
- Learners can start at Lesson 1 and continue through all eight in one sitting, resume later, or practice any lesson independently.
- Added a final **Mixed Mastery** challenge covering all eight parts of speech.
- Course progress, best lesson scores, mixed-challenge score, and resume position persist locally.

## Grade pathway

- Grade 1: nouns and verbs.
- Grade 2: adjectives and pronouns.
- Grade 3: adverbs, prepositions, and conjunctions.
- Grade 4: interjections plus mixed identification.
- Grades 5–6: word function, agreement, editing, and sentence analysis.
- The dedicated course intentionally allows any learner to study all eight parts of speech while keeping the normal Grammar curriculum grade-appropriate.

## Learning integration

- Parts of Speech Lab questions use the existing Grammar mastery system rather than a duplicate progress model.
- Added explicit Interjections skill metadata under the `parts-of-speech` mastery domain.
- Added fixed-question course queues to the core battle engine so curated lessons remain curated and do not get replaced by unrelated adaptive/review questions.
- Course rounds retain the standard Pause, Back, Home, Restart, Android Back, audio, Glorp reaction, and Professor-hosted question behavior.

## Presentation

- Added a dedicated eight-lesson course map, lesson teaching panel, grade-path strip, progress meter, examples, sentence examples, and course continuation flow.
- Added a bespoke Parts of Speech Lab icon and direct activity card.
- Updated the home activity count to reflect the expanded product.
