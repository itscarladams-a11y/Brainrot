# Brainrot Academy v0.16.1 — Logic Lab: Glorp's Lost & Found

## New activity
- Added **Logic Lab** as an enrichment activity, separate from academic mastery.
- Added **Glorp's Lost & Found**, a hidden-school-supplies deduction puzzle.
- One supply is hidden in each colored region; supplies cannot share rows or columns and cannot touch, including diagonally.
- Search mode reveals squares; Mark X mode lets kids eliminate impossible squares.
- Three Brain Power hearts discourage random tapping.
- One optional hint per stage lowers the maximum star rating.
- Grade-aware board sizes scale from 4×4 through 7×7.
- Original uniquely solvable board bank with rotation/reflection variants.
- Separate Logic Lab stage/star progress preserves the integrity of academic mastery data.
